Our Mission

Lunar Magic transforms the night sky into breathtaking aerial artwork with precisely choreographed drone light shows, painting light, motion, and wonder above your event.

A drone light show is a choreographed aerial performance in which a fleet of LED-equipped drones fly in precise formation to create stunning shapes, animations, and displays in the night sky, often synchronized to music. Unlike fireworks, drone shows are reusable, eco-friendly, and endlessly customizable. Every show can be uniquely programmed — your logo, your message, your story — written in light across the sky.

Fully Customizable

Every show can be uniquely built from scratch. Any image you can imagine such as your logo, mascot, or brand colors can be rendered in light across the night sky.

Eco-Friendly

Drones produce zero smoke, zero chemical fallout, and zero fire risk. Fully electric and reusable, a drone show leaves nothing behind but memories and an awe-inspired crowd.

Safe & Flexible

No explosions, no debris, no startled animals or children. Drone shows can be performed in many areas where traditional fireworks are prohibited or impractical.
