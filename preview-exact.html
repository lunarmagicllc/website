Lunar Magic Drone Light Shows

Lunar Magic transforms the night sky into aerial artwork with precisely choreographed LED drones. Shows are customizable, reusable, eco-friendly, safe and silent alternatives to fireworks for public celebrations, corporate events, weddings, sports, festivals, fairs, nonprofits and private events.
